## Not immediate work

- Do not add new data to Higher or Lower or Connections until its source, date and interpretation are clear.
- Do not turn every game into a timed recall quiz; use time only where it improves the skill being tested.
- Do not add photo games without an asset-rights and offline-storage plan.
- Do not promote Historical Geography into Daily without a separate review of content, licensing, identity and persistence.

## New game evaluation

**Map Projection Match** is implemented as a four-task Free Play set. It uses the existing country geometry and D3 projection library, and it remains outside Daily. The local checks establish deterministic task coverage, answer persistence and responsive map rendering; a player study still needs to assess whether the map differences and task wording are clear enough without prior cartography knowledge.

**Population Pyramid Sort** is implemented as a five-round Free Play game with 194 sourced 2023 age profiles, pairwise ordering score, progressively narrower data-derived gaps, answer reveals and an offline-bundled dataset. The scoring rule and the four age bands to sum for the 65+ target are visible before play; the running total is shown out of 1,500. Each anonymous profile has an optional visible table of 17 age-band values rounded to 0.1 percentage point; its effect on the silhouette-reading task needs player review. A note explains why these rounded values may add to a slightly different figure than the unrounded 65+ result. Browser verification covers desktop keyboard focus retention, mobile touch and open-table width. It remains outside Daily. Data coverage and arithmetic are validated; conduct a first-read and replay review before tuning the gap bands or adding more scopes. See the [source and rules review](STUDIES/population-pyramid-sort.md).
