## How to read the evidence

Each study distinguishes:

- **Observed:** what appeared in a live round or the visible game UI during this review.
- **Documented:** what an official page, help panel, FAQ, or catalog says.
- **Interpretation:** a product inference, not a statement from the game developer.
- **Maproom implication:** a candidate change that must still earn its place through local testing.

The public games are dynamic. Catalog counts, ads, consent prompts, daily puzzles, pricing and login gates can change. The date above is the access date, not a promise that the sites remain in the same state.

## Access and limits

- Games were played in guest mode where available. No accounts were created and no paid features were tested.
- WhenTaken and WhereTaken displayed consent prompts covering tracking and precise geolocation processing; no such consent was granted. Their public how-to material and initial game views were reviewed, but no full scored round was submitted.
- GeoGuessr's guest landing page advertised a daily challenge, but the play action led to account creation. The sign-up path was not followed.
- NYT Games was inaccessible behind the browser's safety block. No alternate route or workaround was attempted; its gameplay remains unobserved in this review.
- A page showing a category or feature is not evidence that its play, pricing, or availability was tested. See each deep dive for exact limits.
