# Globle

**Source:** [globle-game.com](https://globle-game.com/) · **Accessed:** 19 November 2026 · **Evidence:** one wrong guess; no solution.

## Observed

The daily mystery-country game accepted a country guess and colored the wrong country on a world map. After guessing France, the nearby-guess list displayed “Closest border: 2,905 km.” The sampled page identified itself as version 1.10.9. The color was presented as a closeness cue, but this review does not infer a specific mathematical meaning for its heat scale.

## Interpretation and Maproom implication

Color feedback turns a text answer into a geographic relationship and gives the next guess a direction. The closest-border distance is a concrete numeric clue; a color alone is less precise but easy to scan. Maproom Riverways now provides distance and compass direction between calculated line centres, with an explicit caveat that this is not closest-course distance. A map-based game should use a measure that is meaningful for its geometry and explain what is being compared.
