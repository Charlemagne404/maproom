# GeoGuessr

**Source:** [GeoGuessr free page](https://www.geoguessr.com/free) · **Accessed:** 19 November 2026 · **Evidence:** guest landing surface only; no gameplay.

## Observed

The public landing page advertised a free daily challenge with five locations. Selecting play led to account creation. No sign-up was attempted, so immersive rounds, map controls, scoring, guest limits and the account experience were not observed.

## Interpretation and Maproom implication

GeoGuessr is a relevant reference for immersive visual location reasoning, but its guest entry was not available to this review. Do not infer details from a headline or another platform's photos. If Maproom investigates an immersive mode, it needs its own openly licensed imagery and must disclose the difference between a landmark clue and a street-level photo. Current map games can continue to use bundled boundaries and local data without adding third-party image services.
