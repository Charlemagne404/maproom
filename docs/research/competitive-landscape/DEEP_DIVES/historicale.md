# Historicale

**Source:** [historicale.com](https://historicale.com/) · **Accessed:** 19 November 2026 · **Evidence:** one daily Timeline round completed; alternate mode reviewed.

## Observed

Timeline Mode presented six event cards to drag into oldest-to-newest order. The reviewed daily board included a railway, World War II, political party, film debut and spaceflight events. The round finished at 4/6; the result highlighted positions that were incorrect and reported the score. A one-day streak indicator and replayable activity were visible.

## Documented

Help described two modes: a daily six-event timeline, with correctly placed events highlighted, and a Before/After format where one event is placed and the player chooses whether another happened before or after it. The second mode was documented but not played.

## Interpretation and Maproom implication

The two formats turn historical knowledge into different actions: ordering several events versus making pairwise temporal comparisons. Results can explain the correct placement and let players inspect the set. Maproom Historical Geography currently asks for a year from a reconstructed political-boundary snapshot and includes citations. A curated sequence mode is plausible only if map differences are distinguishable and each map remains clearly labeled as a reconstruction, not an archival image. Keep Historical Geography secondary and outside Daily while the content and license remain specialized.
