# Sporcle

**Source:** [Sporcle](https://www.sporcle.com/) and [Countries of the World](https://www.sporcle.com/games/g/world) · **Accessed:** 19 November 2026 · **Evidence:** four answers entered in a timed round; the round was ended early.

## Observed

The selected quiz had 197 answers and a 15-minute timer. Correct country names typed into the field caused answers to appear on the map; the first four accepted answers were Canada, United States, Mexico and Brazil. After ending the round, the page showed 4/197, about 2%, an average score of 68%, and the full map labels. The quiz page displayed 46.4 million plays, a 4.8 rating and a recent update date. These are page metrics, not independent quality measurements.

Sporcle offers map, clickable, grid, order, picture, multiple-choice and other quiz formats. Optional cookie processing was rejected; the site presented a continue-without-ads action, which was used without disabling the browser's ad blocker.

## Interpretation and Maproom implication

The rapid retrieval loop and visible map coverage make large recall sets motivating for players who already know the material. The timer can also exclude slower readers and does not suit every learning goal. Maproom should keep timed practice optional and provide a meaningful untimed path. A high public play count signals activity on that page, not evidence that its format is right for every audience.
