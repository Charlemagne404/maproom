# Seterra

**Source:** [Seterra](https://www.seterra.com/) and the [US 50 States quiz](https://www.geoguessr.com/vgp/3003) · **Accessed:** 19 November 2026 · **Evidence:** one guest quiz answer.

## Observed

The current Seterra landing surface was hosted under GeoGuessr branding and advertised more than 400 quizzes, 41 languages, world and regional political geography, physical geography and printables. In the US 50 States quiz, the player was prompted for a state and could use map selection or typed input. Correct Illinois advanced the count from 0/50 to 1/50 and displayed a 100% result for that answer. The quiz was playable as a guest; saving high scores required login.

## Interpretation and Maproom implication

Breadth is useful when quizzes remain organized by subject, region and interaction. Direct map selection gives immediate practice, while typed recall provides a different challenge. Maproom's Explore and practice controls already support this distinction across games. Seterra's catalog size should not be treated as a target; measure first-read clarity, useful feedback and replay before adding similar regional quizzes.
