# TimeGuessr

**Source:** [timeguessr.com](https://timeguessr.com/) · **Accessed:** 19 November 2026 · **Evidence:** one five-round guest game; one result reviewed.

## Observed

One round presented a historical photograph, a year slider and a map pin. The sampled photo was documented as a 1943 scene in The Hague, and the result separated the location score from the year score (about 3,083 location points and 1,592 year points, for 4,678/10,000 overall). The result also showed photo context and attribution information. This is a single round within a five-round session, not a survey of the content library.

## Interpretation and Maproom implication

Scoring the two estimates separately makes it clear whether a player recognized the place, the period, or both. The reveal can teach through source context as well as numeric error. Maproom already uses map placement and dated historical boundary data but does not have a licensed photographic library. A future image mode must budget for rights, attribution and offline delivery. Existing city marker placement is the more data-ready way to practice geographic estimation.
