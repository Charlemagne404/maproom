**Source:** [geoconnections.net](https://geoconnections.net/) · **Accessed:** 19 November 2026 · **Evidence:** active board inspected; no group solved.

## Observed

The live board contained sixteen country tiles and four visible lives. The task was to find four groups of four. The first view showed no category labels, and the page exposed an archive/daily framing. This session did not solve a group or verify the animation and result state.

## Documented

The site's FAQ says that a board has a single intended solution and mentions overlapping attributes as deliberate traps. It describes the format as inspired by Only Connect and NYT Connections, and lists archive and daily play. These are developer statements, not independently verified outcomes.

## Interpretation and Maproom implication

The pressure point is category ambiguity: multiple true relationships can be present among sixteen correct countries, but only one grouping should be defensible as the intended answer. Maproom's Geography Connections content is authored and checked against known country facts and alternate quartets. Keep that standard; do not replace it with a larger but weaker random set. The current library has 24 boards, so replay frequency is a valid research question, but expansion should follow human review of difficulty, decoys and source wording.
