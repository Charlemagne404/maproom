# Geozee

**Sources:** [Geozee](https://geozee.earth/) · [official About page](https://geozee.earth/about) · **Accessed:** 19 November 2026 · **Evidence:** one complete guest daily round and the post-game optimal-assignment view; official rules and in-game Category Atlas reviewed.

## Documented

Geozee describes a daily puzzle with nine countries and nine categories. Each country is assigned to a different category, and each category is used once. A placement earns a category-specific score from 0 to 100; the daily total is the sum of the nine placements. The official rules and Category Atlas expose the scoring rule and data source for each category. The game identified itself as beta during this review.

## Observed

The consent screen offered optional tracking and precise-geolocation processing. I rejected optional consent, used the guest daily mode, and did not create an account. Placements were permanent: after a category was assigned to a country, neither could be reused. Each placement displayed its score and rule, making every choice affect the categories still available to later countries.

The daily sequence contained Venezuela, Belgium, Netherlands, Fiji, Cyprus, Bhutan, Niger, Burundi and Russia. My completed assignment scored **718/782**:

The post-game screen reported 7,732 players and placed this result in the top 16%. In the optimal view, the nine displayed card scores sum to 782. At the same time, its header still showed **718/782** and **best 718**. I record the visible mismatch as a result-screen observation; the optimal card assignment is clear, but the aggregate header did not reflect it in that view.

The Category Atlas listed 38 category types. Its Arable Land entry cited World Bank indicator `AG.LND.ARBL.ZS` (arable land as a percentage of land area) and described higher values as better. In the displayed optimal assignment, Burundi received 51 for that category and Russia received 7. This single puzzle does not establish a data or formula defect.

## Interpretation

The central decision is a constrained allocation problem: an attractive category used now may be the only high-scoring fit for a later country, and placements cannot be revised. The optimal reveal makes that trade-off inspectable after the round. In this sample, using Horizontal Stripes on Venezuela scored 100 but left Russia with 7 for Arable Land; the optimal allocation moved Horizontal Stripes to Russia, gave Venezuela 80 for Colourful Flag, and placed Burundi in Arable Land for 51.

Geozee and Maproom Geography Grid test related but distinct skills. Geozee assigns a one-use category to each country; Geography Grid asks for a distinct country at each intersection of two criteria. One Geozee session gives no evidence that Maproom needs this assignment mechanic or a grid change.

## Maproom implication

Keep the current Geography Grid work focused on clear clue definitions, category variety, and repetition in actual play. Treat irreversible category allocation as a separate concept if Maproom players show interest in a strategy-oriented format. Any trial should show each pairing's scoring rule before commitment and make the final allocation and its total agree in the result view. No Maproom gameplay change follows from this single competitor round.

## Limits

This was one daily puzzle, one guest session, and one player's score. The ranking is the page's 19 November snapshot and does not characterize typical performance. The observed optimal-view header mismatch may be specific to this presentation state. No player interviews, repeated sessions, source-data audit or accessibility assessment were conducted.
