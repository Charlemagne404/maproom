# World Geography Games

**Source:** [world-geography-games.com](https://world-geography-games.com/) and [Countries of the World quiz](https://world-geography-games.com/en/countries_world.html) · **Accessed:** 19 November 2026 · **Evidence:** catalog and live canvas quiz inspected; answer interaction not verified.

## Observed

The homepage grouped broad topics including countries, capitals, flags, continents, oceans, latitude/longitude, rivers, seas, islands, mountains, climate and ecology, with eight language choices visible. The countries quiz opened a world map and a prompt for Kuwait. The initial score was 0/25 with no attempts. A click attempt did not register in the tested browser, and the map was rendered as a canvas with limited accessibility-tree detail. No answer, score change or completion is claimed.

## Interpretation and Maproom implication

The subject breadth is a useful discovery model: physical geography and coordinate work can live beside political quizzes. The failed click means we cannot assess response quality or gameplay balance. Maproom's use of labeled, keyboard-operable alternatives is a strength to retain when adding map-first modes. A canvas-based map needs its own accessibility evaluation; visual interaction alone does not prove equivalent keyboard or screen-reader access.
