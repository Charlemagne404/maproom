# GeoGrid

**Source:** [geogridgame.com](https://www.geogridgame.com/) · **Accessed:** 19 November 2026 · **Evidence:** live board and rules inspected; one answer attempt, no complete round.

## Observed

The browser showed a 3×3 world grid. Each square represented an intersection of a row clue and column clue. The visible rules offered a ten-guess challenge or unlimited play. The board also surfaced rarity-style percentages and share/1v1 affordances. One answer attempt was made, but this review did not establish its acceptance or complete enough of the grid to assess full scoring.

## Interpretation and Maproom implication

The reusable pattern is a compact intersection board where the player selects a cell, finds a country satisfying both clues, and manages a finite budget. A percentage can add a social or rarity layer, but it needs a defensible sample and must not be mistaken for geography correctness.

Maproom Geography Grid already asks for nine distinct countries across two criteria, verifies solvability and reports candidate counts. The opportunity is category quality and variety, not simply increasing board count. Maproom should continue to source and define criteria, avoid arbitrary scarcity percentages, and make duplicate or invalid answers understandable. No claim is made about GeoGrid's full validation behavior from the partial session.
