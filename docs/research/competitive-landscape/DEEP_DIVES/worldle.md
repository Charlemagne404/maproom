# Worldle

**Source:** [worldle.teuteuf.fr](https://worldle.teuteuf.fr/) · **Accessed:** 19 November 2026 · **Evidence:** one incorrect country guess; no full solution.

## Observed

The round showed an unlabeled country silhouette, a six-attempt budget, an autocomplete guess field, and visible practice/archive options. Guessing France returned a distance of about 4,194 km, a downward direction arrow and a displayed 79% proximity figure. The tool revealed a remaining-attempt count after the guess. The meaning of the percentage was not independently established, so this note does not interpret it.

## Interpretation and Maproom implication

The strong loop is that an incorrect answer still gives the player a spatial decision for the next guess. Distance and direction can teach relative geography; the game can also keep the target hidden until the attempt budget is used. Maproom Country Outlines already has its own distance/direction and later regional clues. Riverways adopted a related principle using calculated centres of two generalized line features, with explicit wording that the distance is not a river-course minimum.

The Worldle percentage should not be copied without knowing its scale and calibration. This single round does not establish how the bonus rounds, all difficulty modes, archive or unlimited play behave.
