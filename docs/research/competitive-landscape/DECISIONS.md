# Research and product decisions

Updated 19 November 2026. This log records choices for the continuous quality mission, not final product policy.

| Decision | Basis | Consequence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Improve a current game's feedback before adding another quiz. | The mission prioritizes game quality. Riverways had a clear first-miss information gap, while another game would add more content and maintenance. | Add spatial miss feedback in Riverways first; defer new game code. |
| Compare generalized river-line centres, and label the measure. | Natural Earth centerlines have no verified network or flow graph in this build. Centre-to-centre distance and direction are computable; shortest course distance is not. | Round distance to 50 km, report direction, disclose that it is not nearest-course distance, and test all authored questions. |
| Show the target-centre country after the second miss. | Existing country polygons can locate all 134 authored question centres in the current question pool. The clue is local and deterministic. | Provide one broad spatial comparison before the more specific country clue. |
| Preserve all 24 Connections boards and their standards. | The content guide requires authored, sourceable and uniqueness-audited groups. The test timeout came from repeated whole-catalog scans in the audit, not gameplay generation. | Optimize exact checking over the 16 board tiles; do not lower test timeout or change boards. |
| Keep the test timeout at its normal value. | After the audit optimization, the formerly slow 36-board scenario passed with the normal Vitest timeout. | Future content work remains covered by the same run-time check. |
| Select Map Projection Match as the next Free Play game. | The task offers a distinct visual map-reading action, uses already bundled country geometry and `d3-geo`, and can explain four common projection tradeoffs without external data. USGS and D3 primary documentation supports the property descriptions. | Implement a deterministic four-task set for Equal Earth, Mercator, Equirectangular and Orthographic; keep it outside Daily until player clarity and difficulty are assessed. |
| Balance only the expanded Grid Free Play pool by observed generator usage. | The old 240-board pool had 345 distinct row/column category pairs and a P90 intersection size of 32; a weighted Free Play draw produced 371 pairs and P90 27 while retaining 240 valid boards. The most-used clue changed from 76 to 77 appearances, so this is not uniform balance. | Keep the Daily generator and identity path unchanged; retain regression thresholds for pool size, category/pair variety and intersection size. Treat human difficulty and perceived repetition as unmeasured. |
| Report route efficiency after Border Route accepts a complete set. | The user can build a longer valid route, but the result only showed the path and points. The shortest benchmark was visible during play but disappeared behind the results summary. | State the chosen border count and the shortest count, with the difference in intermediate stops; keep arbitrary-order entry and all valid routes. |
| Name the set difference after Neighbour Challenge checks a guess. | Counts alone did not teach which selected countries were correct, which target neighbors were missed, or which countries were extra; the selected chips disappear after checking. | Show found, missed and incorrect country names in the status reveal. Keep full-set scoring and the existing brief feedback/advance flow. |
| Do not treat an advertised mode as tested or free. | Geocore's catalog includes Windows-only, Pro and account labels; GeoGuessr's guest play redirected to signup. | Catalog exact observed labels and state access uncertainty per mode. |
| Do not grant third-party precise-location or advertising consent for research. | WhenTaken and WhereTaken consent screens listed precise geolocation and ad processing; a scored round could not be responsibly inspected without deciding those preferences. | Record public instructions and initial UI, not full-round results. |
| Do not route around the NYT browser safety block. | The user's research instructions require respecting access restrictions. | Mark NYT gameplay unobserved and select other references for direct play. |
| Do not add a new game to Daily automatically. | Current Daily has an explicit allowlist and deterministic puzzle identity. | Future games require a separate content, persistence, fairness and schedule review. |
| Defer Country Mosaic after direct competitor play. | Geocore Geo Puzzle already asks players to rebuild a world map from country pieces and provides snap feedback and geographic hints. The candidate's current core loop would repeat that action without a distinct learning goal. | Keep the idea documented but inactive; revisit only if research supports a different geographic skill and accessible interaction. |
| Keep CCKP ERA5 as the accessible Climate Twins prototype source, subject to its published additional terms. | The ERA5 catalog record identifies CC BY 4.0, the collection documents 1991–2020 monthly climatologies at 0.25°, and the inspected files have consistent period, grid, variable and unit metadata. CCKP adds mandatory dispute-resolution and attribution requirements. The full catalog has 241 capital-marked places; 127 pass the conservative same-country land-cell and under-1,000 m screen. | A curated Free Play prototype may use ERA5-derived profiles if it pins the files and includes World Bank/provider attribution and the CCKP terms URL. A direct Copernicus ECV route is available under CC BY 4.0 if CDS access is configured. Keep CRU TS and WorldClim held; do not add Climate Twins to Daily before player review. See [the source study](STUDIES/climate-twins-source-review.md). |
| Defer Terrain Profile as an ordering game. | GSI provides a reusable, attributed national mountain-height list, but close examples include ties and 3–10 m gaps. More importantly, summit sorting repeats Maproom's Latitude Ladder and Population Pyramid Sort and overlaps competitor ranking/elevation-comparison modes. | Do not prototype the current order-by-height loop. Revisit only with a distinct terrain-reading interaction and its own source review. Keep DEM samples out of the answer key; see [the source review](STUDIES/terrain-profile-source-review.md). |
| Add life expectancy at birth to Higher or Lower Free Play only. | World Bank WDI has an exact 2023 observation for 194/195 Standard identities and a distinct human-geography learning goal. The current ratio-based Close Calls band covers nearly every pair, so the new measure uses absolute year gaps. | Show the year, source, and newborn-estimate meaning; exclude missing values without fallback. Keep Daily on population, area and density until players assess clarity and challenge. |
| Keep photo-based games as research only. | Rights, attribution, consent, offline packaging and image provenance need dedicated review. | Do not reuse competitor photographs or build image-round content from scraped pages. |
| Prototype Watershed Match from WBD's own downstream links. | The USGS 2025 WBD HUC12 API includes geometry and `ToHUC` in one CC0 collection. In three sampled regions, 344/344 captured downstream targets shared a boundary with their source; 95 transitions also had two clean adjacent alternatives after requiring unmodified subwatersheds. MapGuesser's observed feature round already uses pin placement and distance feedback; Riverways covers river identification. | Build a curated U.S.-only Free Play prototype from the 2025 WBD snapshot alone; avoid the Mainstems/WBD cross-snapshot join. Keep it out of Daily. Review candidate question maps and test the downstream action with players before changing the concept's long-term status. See [the source study](STUDIES/watershed-match-source-review.md). |
| Keep the Watershed Match prototype limited until editorial and player review. | The pilot bundles 60 questions from a name-similarity shortlist, with 118 HUC12 polygons in three local systems. Browser checks cover the five-question loop, restore/replay, mobile, dark theme and offline load; they do not establish question fairness or comprehension. | Keep it Free Play only and out of Daily. Review the full question set and its map frames; measure player understanding and perceived difficulty before expanding national coverage. The bundled data is 383,428 bytes (36,752 gzip). See [the source study](STUDIES/watershed-match-source-review.md). |
