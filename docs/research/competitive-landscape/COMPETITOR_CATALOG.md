# Competitor catalog

Reviewed 19 November 2026. This is a dated inventory of public surfaces, not a complete feature or pricing audit. See the [deep dives](DEEP_DIVES/README.md) before drawing conclusions about play quality.

## Teuteuf Games

The official [Teuteuf Games portfolio](https://teuteuf.fr/) listed 19 games on the access date:

| Game | Publicly described core action | Maproom relevance |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Worldle | Identify a country from its silhouette; the official page advertises eight bonus rounds, and wrong guesses return distance, direction and a proximity indicator. | Country Outlines and informative miss feedback. |
| Travle | Build a country path between endpoints; the game evaluates routes against shortest-path expectations. | Border Route and route-quality feedback. |
| Geozee | Assign countries to categories and maximize the resulting category score. | Geography Grid category design and meaningful trade-offs. |
| Flagle | Guess a flag in six attempts; the official page advertises five bonus rounds with country trivia. | Flag Study progression and optional learning. |
| GeoGrid | Fill a 3×3 grid by naming a country that satisfies the row and column clues. | Geography Grid. |
| GeoConnections | Find links among countries, cities, landmarks and other places, and organize them into four categories. | Geography Connections editorial standards. The sampled live board used country tiles. |
| Statele | Identify a US state from its silhouette; bonus trivia is advertised. | State and subdivision play. |
| Mapster | Draw country shapes from memory. | A possible alternative input style, with accessibility and scoring questions. |
| HexHunt | Find seven requested locations on a hexagonal world map. | Map-reading and compact touch targets. |
| Historicale | Order historical events on a timeline or answer before/after comparisons. | Historical Geography's curated timeline potential. |
| WhenTaken | Place a map pin and estimate the year of a photograph. | Marker-first spatial and historical reasoning. |
| WhenTaken Movies | Estimate the year and location of a movie scene. | Media-based geography; image rights and scene metadata would be required. |
| WhereTaken | Identify where a photograph was taken by placing a map pin. | Photo-based location estimation; significant licensing and consent constraints. |
| WhereTaken Classic | Identify the country or region shown in a photograph. | Photo-based regional recognition. |
| WhereTaken US | Identify the US state shown in a photograph. | Region-scale image recognition. |
| Angle | Guess the angle in four guesses or fewer. | Spatial reasoning; exact round details were not independently sampled. |
| Searchle | Guess a secret word from a Google prompt in five guesses. | Search as a discovery mechanic; exact round details were not independently sampled. |
| Emovi | Guess a movie from emoji clues, with three guesses and two hints. | A non-map clue format that may support geography-adjacent content. |
| Francele | Identify a French department from its map image; the official page advertises six bonus rounds. | Subnational geography and regional pools. |

The catalog descriptions above are official portfolio summaries. Only the individually documented browser-played rounds in the deep dives count as direct gameplay evidence.

The gaps at 070–071 appear in the current numbering; the catalog still reports 85 modes. The page labels **28** modes no-Pro and **11** no-account. These are catalog labels, not an independent test of every guest or paid flow. Six modes are explicitly Windows-only. Pricing and account gates vary by mode and settings; availability of a catalog tile alone does not imply a browser-playable free experience.

The live [Play page](https://www.geocore.fun/play) separately showed 169 games and 2,200+ quizzes during a second inspection on 19 November. An earlier same-day capture showed 168; the public total can change as the page updates. The official [Geocore 2.0 announcement](https://www.geocore.fun/blog/geocore-2-0) described 73 games at its October 2026 release. These counts describe different surfaces or times; the current catalog did not expose a version string. This report treats 85 as the observed Classic catalog count and 169 as the later Play-page total, without claiming a single unified product total.

The catalog describes 81 modes as coverage-scored and four heatmap modes as proximity-scored. Its published coverage score uses map coverage and accuracy, with completion, hint and dataset modifiers; heatmaps use proximity and guess efficiency. Tutorials, settings and scoring explanations appear on individual mode pages. A guest browser session of Click Country showed a map-based question counter and immediate answer reveal; it was not a representative test of all 85 modes. No separate beta label or complete per-mode entitlement matrix was visible in the captured catalog, so unmarked modes remain unclassified here.
