# Data sources, licensing and attribution

The exact retrieval timestamp, source URL, input SHA-256, transformation list and license URL for the current build are recorded in `public/data/data-manifest.json`. The live Data Sources page reads that manifest and `region-index.json`; the latter records boundary ID, represented year, source, primary source URL, license evidence, provider URL and release reference for each included ADM1 country package.

## Country boundaries

Natural Earth 1:50m Admin 0 Countries supplies world-map geometry, and its 1:10m layer supplies the detailed Country Outlines silhouettes. Both are public domain and credited as “Made with Natural Earth.” Features retain Polygon/MultiPolygon rings and are normalized to Maproom identity codes. Explicit component anchors separate territories that Natural Earth groups under a parent, including the Caribbean Netherlands, French overseas regions, Tokelau, Svalbard and Jan Mayen, and Christmas and Cocos Islands. The app stores both layers as shared-border TopoJSON quantized on a 1e6 coordinate grid and decodes them when a map game needs them. Conversion checks retained every source vertex and feature type; the grid rounds coordinates by at most about 20 m in latitude and 40 m in longitude at the equator. Natural Earth's generalized boundaries and conventions around small islands and disputed areas remain visible in the coverage notes. [Natural Earth terms](https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/).

## Country facts and identities

The `mledoze/countries` catalog supplies ISO codes, common and official names, alternate and native spellings, continent/subregion, capital names, area, landlocked state and neighbor codes. Maproom also adds five Natural Earth geographic identities without ISO country codes: Clipperton Island (`CLP`), Ashmore and Cartier Islands (`ATC`), the Coral Sea Islands (`CSI`), and the Akrotiri (`WSB`) and Dhekelia (`ESB`) Sovereign Base Areas. Those three-letter keys are Maproom identity codes, not claims of ISO assignment. Their parent relationships are explicit. They have no separate flag identity and are excluded from Flag Study to avoid duplicate or misleading flag answers. The downloaded upstream license is kept at `public/data/COUNTRY-CATALOG-ODbL-1.0.txt`; this database and its adapted catalog are subject to the Open Database License 1.0, including its attribution and share-alike requirements for adapted databases. The manifest stores the source file checksum. [Upstream project and license](https://github.com/mledoze/countries) · [ODbL 1.0](https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/1-0/).

## Population

Country population quiz facts use the World Bank World Development Indicators series `SP.POP.TOTL` for the 2023 observation year where a non-null value is available, joined by ISO3 code. The build falls back to the `population` field in the `mledoze/countries` catalog when that World Bank observation is absent; Maproom records no observation year for those fallback values. This is a pinned data build, not a live feed. World Bank open data is generally under CC BY 4.0 with the Bank's dataset terms; the manifest records the exact API response hash, API last-updated date, attribution and source URL. [World Bank Indicators API](https://datahelpdesk.worldbank.org/knowledgebase/articles/889392-about-the-indicators-api) · [World Bank data license](https://datacatalog.worldbank.org/public-licenses).

Higher or Lower Free Play also uses the World Bank World Development Indicators series `SP.DYN.LE00.IN` for the exact 2023 observation year, joined by ISO3 code with no catalog fallback. Values are in years and represent life expectancy at birth: the expected years a newborn would live if mortality patterns at birth remained the same. The country catalog stores the value as `lifeExpectancyYears2023`; absent observations remain `null`. The World Bank indicator page lists CC BY 4.0 and names UN World Population Prospects, national statistical offices and Eurostat as source providers. The data manifest records the API response hash, last-updated date, attribution, and transformations. This is a population-level estimate, not an individual prediction or direct measure of healthcare quality. [Indicator and source metadata](https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/SP.DYN.LE00.IN) · [World Bank dataset terms](https://data.worldbank.org/summary-terms-of-use).

Population Pyramid Sort uses the 2023 World Bank WDI age-band series for 17 five-year cohorts, male and female population totals, and the separate `SP.POP.65UP.TO.ZS` indicator. Each sex-specific cohort share is weighted by that sex's population total to put both sides of the chart on a shared whole-population denominator. The 65+ band sum agrees with the separate WDI indicator within 0.00008 percentage points across complete profiles. There are 194 complete Standard-country profiles; Vatican City has no complete profile and is omitted with no fallback. The bundle is local and offline-cached. Its manifest records CC BY 4.0 and the World Bank dataset terms, with WDI attribution and the underlying UN World Population Prospects 2024 Revision. See the [source and rules review](research/competitive-landscape/STUDIES/population-pyramid-sort.md), [WDI API](https://datahelpdesk.worldbank.org/knowledgebase/articles/889392-about-the-indicators-api), and [World Bank dataset terms](https://data.worldbank.org/summary-terms-of-use).

## First-level administrative boundaries

The build uses the geoBoundaries current gbOpen ADM1 index and the provider's simplified per-country GeoJSON. In the current build, 198 country packages were included, with 3,236 named divisions. Each package is quantized and converted to TopoJSON at a 1e-5 degree coordinate grid and is loaded only on demand. The public metadata records its primary provider, boundary release, represented year and license evidence; where geoBoundaries points to a separately licensed primary source, its terms still apply. Do not infer uniform coverage or license terms from the global count. [geoBoundaries API and metadata](https://www.geoboundaries.org/api.html). The generated [coverage table](DATA_COVERAGE.md) lists every country package and its stated license.

## Cities and capitals

GeoNames `cities500.zip` provides populated-place names, alternate names, coordinates, ISO country and first-level codes, population and feature codes. The full app gazetteer includes 34,456 records: populated places with population of 50,000 or more, plus national and first-level seats even when smaller. Capitals & Cities and the six-game Daily city pool keep this full file and its existing question generation, preserving current Daily identities. Distance & Direction and Latitude Ladder load a derived, order-preserving file of 12,405 places at or above 50,000; this keeps their seeded eligible sequences intact while avoiding records neither game can use. In the full bundle, 828 records with codes `PPLH`, `PPLQ`, `PPLW` or `PPLX` meet the 50,000 threshold, and 420 meet Latitude Ladder's 100,000 threshold. GeoNames defines these as historical, abandoned, destroyed, or section records rather than current whole settlements. Free Play pools in Capitals & Cities, Distance & Direction and Latitude Ladder exclude them. The package is released under CC BY 4.0 and attributed as GeoNames. Capital names come from the country catalog; capital coordinates are joined from GeoNames national-capital (`PPLC`) records where available. [GeoNames export and terms](https://www.geonames.org/export/), [GeoNames feature-code definitions](https://www.geonames.org/export/codes.html).

## River centerlines

Riverways uses 148 named `River` features from Natural Earth's 1:50m Rivers and Lake Centerlines dataset, pinned to `natural-earth-vector` commit `ca96624a56bd078437bca8184e78163e5039ad19`. The build keeps scale ranks 1–4 and only the name and rank properties needed by the game. Segments sharing a normalized English name are combined into one question; ambiguous aliases are omitted from answer matching. The data is public domain and loaded on demand. It is generalized for world-scale recognition, is not complete hydrography, and must not be used for navigation. The source checksum, transformation list and generated `rivers.json` checksum are in the data manifest. [Natural Earth 1:50m physical vectors](https://www.naturalearthdata.com/downloads/50m-physical-vectors/) · [pinned river-centerline source](https://github.com/nvkelso/natural-earth-vector/blob/ca96624a56bd078437bca8184e78163e5039ad19/geojson/ne_50m_rivers_lake_centerlines.geojson) · [Natural Earth terms](https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/).

## Watershed subunits

The limited Watershed Match Free Play pilot uses the 2025 USGS Hydrologic Geospatial Fabric collection `wbd12_20250107`. Its HUC12 features provide both polygon geometry and `ToHUC`, the neighboring unit that naturally receives the majority of flow. The build samples three fixed bounding boxes in the Appalachians, Columbia and Susquehanna areas, then keeps unmodified subwatersheds with numeric 12-digit `ToHUC` targets that share a polygon boundary with their source. It selects two distinct adjacent alternatives per question, bundles only referenced geometry and quantizes the TopoJSON to a 20,000-step grid. The current asset contains 60 questions and 118 polygons and is cached for offline play.

The first sample window crosses four HUC8 basins, so the player-facing sample label is “Appalachians”; none of the three local windows is a complete basin or national coverage. Ocean, Canada, Mexico, closed-basin and other exceptional outlet codes are excluded. A reveal arrow shows the stored relation schematically and is not a river trace or navigation path. The source manifest records CC0 1.0 terms linked by the API collection, attribution to USGS and the Natural Resources Conservation Service, the asset checksum and transformations. See the [official 2025 HUC12 collection](https://api.water.usgs.gov/fabric/pygeoapi/collections/wbd12_20250107?f=html), the [source and gameplay review](research/competitive-landscape/STUDIES/watershed-match-source-review.md), and the [first-read player pilot](research/competitive-landscape/STUDIES/watershed-match-player-pilot.md).

## Flags

Flag art is bundled from `flag-icons` and keyed by ISO 3166-1 alpha-2 code. The package is MIT-licensed and is pinned through `package-lock.json`; its CSS and SVG assets are shipped locally, so gameplay does not call a flag service. [Project and license](https://github.com/lipis/flag-icons).

## Historical maps, states and empires

The expanded Historical Geography and Historical States & Empires games use a curated set of 41 Maproom-generated regional and global SVG views from [Cliopatria v0.2.1](https://github.com/Seshat-Global-History-Databank/cliopatria/releases/tag/v0.2.1), a Seshat Global History Databank project, at commit `5f433377139a6eeaeacbdc894b70f8805b72c8b5`. The source is licensed [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). The exact source archive SHA-256 is `e10a4e429fca708788ff9e7572a95fce801fae55852d217cffabc1c59cd4eed4`; the extracted GeoJSON SHA-256 is `fc6f0d1e4cc42f45da83522b3696db6f2cc85ec0d12259a85c89b31347b8fc0a`. The raw source archive is not shipped. Each included map links its selected polity overview when available and describes its source interval, contextual limits and curated frame.

These 41 selected views cover sample years 3300 BCE to 1900 CE across 20 named frames, including regional windows and a global view. They do not form a complete world map series or an exhaustive inventory of societies. Cliopatria contains `POLITY` and `RELATION` features, varied source intervals, overlapping political entities and incomplete regional coverage. Maproom selects only `POLITY` geometries; it does not treat blank space as uninhabited or the selected outlines as a partition of the world. The displayed answer interval is the inclusive source interval shared by the geometry used for that view. Cliopatria's date ranges can be broad and reflect one scholarly reconstruction without polygon-level confidence values. The map is not an archival map, legal boundary, or precise event-date claim.

The generated SVGs clip, orient, simplify and render source geometries into each curated regional or global window. Every asset hash, release identifier, source interval, clue and uncertainty note is checked against the built manifest. Source records, seed choices and the reproducible builder are in [`docs/data/historical/RESEARCH.md`](data/historical/RESEARCH.md), [`atlas-seeds.json`](data/historical/atlas-seeds.json), and [`build-historical-atlas.mjs`](../scripts/data/build-historical-atlas.mjs). The historical map generator and assets retain the source attribution and CC BY 4.0 terms. The separate legacy CShapes game remains a distinct data pack under its recorded CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 terms.

## Historical flags

Historical Flags contains 20 locally bundled original vector redrawings across 19 political entities. Each item records its historical period, region, primary source, explanation and a visible `documented` or `reconstructed` artwork status. Eight drawings are explicitly marked reconstructed; this identifies uncertainty in the pictured design, not uncertainty about the existence of a flag. Where period or design claims need more than one reference, the additional citations are included in the source manifest. The flag source index is available on the Data Sources page and each round reveal.

The redrawn SVG artwork is original Maproom work and is licensed [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/), with attribution to the cited basis for each design. Flag source text and references are not bundled images. A reconstruction is a learning illustration and should not be treated as an archival specimen or proof that every historical variant looked identical. Seed records, citations, period notes and the builder are in [`flag-seeds.json`](data/historical/flag-seeds.json), [`RESEARCH.md`](data/historical/RESEARCH.md), and [`build-historical-flags.mjs`](../scripts/data/build-historical-flags.mjs).

## Rebuilding and reviewing data

Run `npm run data:sync` to download current sources, update every checksum and retrieval date, regenerate country/city/boundary files, and refresh `docs/DATA_COVERAGE.md`. `npm run data:geometries` rebuilds only the country geometry and catalog artifacts from the cached Natural Earth 1:50m, 1:10m and mledoze source files. Explicit Maproom parent-territory relationships assign bundled components to distinct territory identities. The country catalog and Natural Earth URLs track upstream branches, so a later rebuild can change names, facts or geometry. Review both generated reports and source-license evidence before distributing a refreshed dataset. No boundary or fact is invented to fill gaps.
