# Game rules and scoring

Maproom has sixteen registered games. Each Daily lineup selects six of eight eligible games: Find the country, Country Outline Quiz, Border Route, Flag Match, Geography Grid, Capitals & Cities, Riverways and Latitude Ladder. Geography Connections, Higher or Lower: Countries, Neighbour Challenge, City Distance & Direction, When Was This Map?, Map Projection Match, Population Pyramid Sort and Watershed Match are Free Play experiences and remain outside the Daily rotation. Daily dates use the time zone in Settings. In-progress daily sessions are saved locally and reopen with the same question sequence; completed results remain available from the puzzle and Statistics.

## Country pools

Country-based practice defaults to **Standard**: the 193 United Nations member states plus the Holy See and the State of Palestine, the two UN non-member observer states. The source catalogue has 255 identities. **Extended** ordinary game pools use 243 of them: Standard plus separately represented territories and additional identities; 12 special geographic records remain catalogued but are withheld from ordinary questions. Parent relationships are recorded where appropriate; Extended membership does not imply independent sovereignty. Kosovo and other places outside Standard are available only in Extended where the game has suitable data. Borders use the catalog's reciprocal land-neighbour graph; maritime claims and disputed boundaries are not adjudicated. See [GEOGRAPHY_ELIGIBILITY.md](GEOGRAPHY_ELIGIBILITY.md) for the inclusion rule and references.

## Find the country

Daily asks for eight countries. Practice offers 10, 20, 50, or unlimited questions, a 60-second round, worldwide or continent scope, and first-level subdivision practice for supported countries. Standard is the default pool; Extended is available in settings. Identify each answer directly on the map; focus a map region and press Enter/Space to select it. There is no country-search answer path. Correct answers earn 100 points; a flag and country name appear with the map result before the next question advances automatically. Optional review puts previously missed countries first. An optional four-try mode is available for country-map practice: each miss shows approximate centre-to-centre distance rounded to 50 km and direction from the guessed country toward the target, and correct answers score 100, 75, 50 or 25 points by attempt. Four misses reveal the answer. One-click practice remains the default; Daily and subdivision practice stay one-click. Subdivision files load only when selected; see [DATA_COVERAGE.md](DATA_COVERAGE.md).

## Country Outline Quiz

Name the country shown by its Natural Earth 1:10m silhouette, or use practice settings for a supported first-level subdivision. Daily uses the Standard catalogue and a versioned deterministic question. Free play has Standard, balanced Extended, and optional Territory focus pools. Easy, Medium and Hard use an exhaustive curated silhouette rating; entries with generic, tiny or widely dispersed outlines are excluded with documented reasons. Eligible questions do not repeat before the selected pool is exhausted, and no rolling set of ten includes more than three Extended entries while Standard candidates remain. If a region and difficulty produce no eligible outlines, the game explains the limitation instead of silently changing settings. Easy (8 guesses), Medium (6), and Hard (4) also change scoring penalties. Rotation is optional. Misses give approximate distance and direction; later hints narrow the region or position. A correct answer starts at 1,000 points and loses 100, 150, or 250 points per earlier miss by difficulty, with a 100-point floor. Exhausting the guesses reveals the answer. An in-progress version 2 daily outline retains its saved target and attempts as it moves to version 3; completed history remains valid.

## Border route

Connect the fixed start and destination with intermediate countries that share recorded land borders. Add any intermediate country from autocomplete in any order. The game treats the selected countries as a set, then uses the reciprocal-neighbor graph to find a continuous ordering that uses each selected country once; the ordered map route appears as soon as the collection is valid. A disconnected collection stays editable. Practice offers world or continent scope and Short (2–4 borders), Standard (3–6), or Long (5–8) targets; when a region has no exact match, generation uses the closest reachable route length. A valid route need not be shortest. Score starts at 1,000, losing 150 points per intermediate country beyond a shortest path and 100 per hint. Duplicate countries and endpoint entries are rejected. Hints name a country on a shortest route.

## Flag Match

Daily gives eight four-choice questions. Practice offers 10, 20, 50, or unlimited questions, region, Standard or Extended country pools, flag-to-country or country-to-flag direction, and typed or multiple-choice answers where applicable. Distractors prefer the same continent, then draw from the selected pool if needed. Optional progressive reveal gives another chance after each of the first two wrong answers, exposing one more equal-width flag slice each time; the first slice's position is balanced across the round. A third wrong answer shows the answer. A wrong selection highlights both the selected option and the correct answer; resolved questions advance automatically after brief feedback. Optional review puts previously missed flags first. Correct answers earn 100 points; missed flag codes are saved in local history.

## Geography grid

Fill all nine crossings with distinct countries that satisfy both the row and column clues. The curated Daily pool retains its existing preset set. Free Play also draws from an expanded criterion pool, including Southern Europe under UN M49, European Union membership, five or more recorded land neighbors, 2023 population from 10–50 million, area over one million km², and capital latitude north or south of the equator. Other criteria cover continent or subregion, landlocked status, coastline, land-neighbor presence, population and area bands, density, and recorded capital coordinates. The expanded criteria do not change Daily rotation or puzzle identity. Southern Europe follows the [UN M49 classification](https://unstats.un.org/unsd/methodology/m49/overview/), including North Macedonia; the EU category uses a fixed 27-state list matching the [EU member countries](https://european-union.europa.eu/principles-countries-history/eu-countries_en) listed on 18 November 2026. Population bands use 2023 observations; area and recorded land neighbors come from Maproom's country catalog; capital-coordinate criteria use the catalogued capital coordinates. A cell is playable only when it has 2–90 eligible candidates, and the generator finds a nine-country distinct-answer assignment before a set can be played. Each selected answer shows whether it satisfies each criterion. Cells with fewer valid answers are worth more: 200 points for two or three answers, 150 for four to eight, and 100 for nine or more. These are rule-based scarcity tiers, not answer-popularity estimates. The daily grid generator is version 3 so older completions remain recognized.

## Capitals & cities

Daily mixes two capital questions with two city-location and two city-identification questions. Practice can focus on capitals, placing a city, or naming a marked city. It offers Standard or Extended country pools, region and population filters, 10, 20, 50, or unlimited questions, and a 60-second round. Capital practice supports country-to-capital and capital-to-country, typed or multiple-choice. Text answers accept catalog aliases. Correct capital and city-name answers earn 100 points. For city placement, click/tap the map or open the secondary coordinate-entry panel; great-circle distance earns up to 1,000 points, falling through distance bands to 100 points beyond 2,500 km. A placement within 250 km counts as accurate. Identification choices are scoped to the marked city's country to avoid same-name ambiguity. Feedback advances to the next question automatically after a brief reveal. The city gazetteer loads on demand.

## Geography Connections

Group sixteen countries into four sets of four. Select four countries at a time to uncover each shared geographic connection; four mistakes end the round. Easy uses familiar Standard countries and clear continent, landlocked, and named-neighbour connections. Medium adds subregions, landlocked subregions, and less obvious named border relationships. Hard uses more specific subregions and supported population or area bands; Extended is available for territory practice. Every board is checked against the category pool for a unique defensible grouping. Solved groups move into coloured rows and their connection is explained. Each group adds 250 points; each incorrect set costs 40 points. “One away” means three selected countries share one intended group. Practice replays with a new seed.

## Higher or Lower: Countries

Compare two countries by population, land area, or population density. Free Play also offers 2023 life expectancy at birth, shown in years with a brief definition. For this measure, Standard pairs differ by at least four years; Close calls differ by one to less than four years. The other measures keep the existing ratio-based difficulty bands. Daily continues to use only population, land area, and population density. Practice offers Standard or Extended country pools, world or continent scope, 10, 20, 50, or unlimited questions, and an optional 60-second round. If a selected scope has fewer eligible comparisons than requested, the game says the round will end after those comparisons. Correct answers earn 100 points plus up to 50 streak points; a wrong answer earns no points and resets the streak. Values and the correct comparison appear after the choice. Population uses the 2023 World Bank series where available, with country-catalog values filling gaps; area comes from the country catalog, and density divides the population value by catalogued area. Life expectancy is the 2023 World Bank WDI estimate at birth, joined by ISO3 with no fallback; it describes expected years under prevailing mortality patterns, not an individual prediction or healthcare ranking. The observation year for catalog population fallbacks is not recorded.

## Neighbour Challenge

Select every country with a recorded reciprocal land border around the target, then check the set. Practice offers Standard or Extended country pools, world or continent scope, all eligible targets or countries with at least five neighbours, 5, 10, 20, or unlimited rounds, and an optional 60-second round. Each target starts at 1,000 points, scaled by the share of neighbours found; every extra selection costs another 100 points. A round counts as correct only when the full set is exact. Answers reveal missed and extra countries. Country search remains available when a place has no map outline.

## City Distance & Direction

Compare two populated places around an origin. Practice covers closest-to-origin, farther north, and farther east of Greenwich. It offers Standard or Extended country pools, continent scope, minimum city population of 50,000, 100,000, 250,000, or 1 million, Standard or Close calls, 10, 20, 50, or unlimited comparisons, and an optional 60-second round. Close distance calls use ratios from 1.01 to 1.35; Standard calls use at least 1.25. For latitude and longitude, Close calls use differences from 0.35° to under 4°, while Standard uses at least 4°. A selected region and population threshold can have fewer unique comparisons than requested; the game explains when that shortens a finite round. No coordinates, distances, bearings, country labels, or other answer metadata are shown until after a choice. Correct answers earn 100 points plus up to 50 streak points; incorrect answers reset the streak. Feedback then reveals coordinates, distance, and bearing. Great-circle distances use the Haversine calculation; north/east comparisons use latitude and signed longitude.

## Riverways

Name the highlighted river trace using autocomplete. There are six distinct guesses; repeated guesses do not consume one. Easy includes Natural Earth scale ranks 1–2, Medium ranks 1–3, and Hard ranks 1–4. After a second wrong guess, feedback gives the country containing the trace's calculated center when one can be identified. A correct answer starts at 1,000 points and loses 160 for each previous wrong answer, with a 200-point floor; using all six guesses reveals the river and scores zero. Practice replay advances through a seeded shuffle before repeating the pool. The game is Free Play only. It uses generalized named centerlines, so a mapped trace may omit small tributaries or channel detail.

## Latitude Ladder

Order five populated places from north to south or west to east; the selected axis appears in the shared Free Play setup. Arrow buttons move each city one slot, so reordering works with touch and keyboard without relying on drag. The pool is worldwide or one continent and includes GeoNames places with population of at least 100,000, excluding historical, abandoned, destroyed and section records. Each correctly placed city is worth 200 points, up to 1,000. After checking, the answer shows each city's signed hemisphere coordinate and the map marks all five true locations. The game is Free Play only. City coverage follows the GeoNames cities500-derived Maproom file and is not a complete city gazetteer.

## When Was This Map?

Study an unlabeled global political-boundary snapshot and enter the year it depicts. The 20-map seeded deck is deterministic for a session and does not repeat an entry until all 20 have appeared. Easy accepts guesses within 20 years, Medium within 8 years, and Hard within 2 years; each boundary is inclusive and the result shows the exact accepted range. These bands are calibrated to the selected 1886–2011 map dates, so one middle-year guess cannot pass most rounds. A closer guess earns more points, from 1,000 at the depicted year to zero at twice the selected tolerance. The reveal gives the depicted year, distance from the guess, a short historical explanation, and expandable date, source and license details.

Free Play also has an **Order three maps** format. Each seeded deck cycles through six curated groups, with at least 22 years between neighboring snapshots. Use the Earlier and Later buttons to arrange the unlabeled maps from oldest to newest; each of the three map pairs in the right order earns one third of the 1,000-point score. The result reveals the ordered dates, historical notes, source links and licensing. The prototype uses existing snapshots only and remains outside Daily while player clarity and challenge are unvalidated.

Each SVG is a Maproom-generated 31 December snapshot from CShapes 2.1, not an archival map published in that year. The initial curated years are 1886, 1898, 1914, 1918, 1920, 1923, 1939, 1945, 1949, 1956, 1960, 1965, 1971, 1975, 1980, 1991, 1993, 2002, 2006 and 2011. Attribution, context links, source hashes and dating caveats are recorded per map. CShapes is licensed CC BY-NC-SA 4.0, so the derived collection is for noncommercial use and adaptations must retain the same license. The game is Free Play only and is not in Daily rotation. Its generalized global boundaries omit subnational detail, many occupation fronts and some disputed or de facto arrangements; see [historical data research](data/historical/RESEARCH.md).

## Map Projection Match

Match each mapping task to one of four rendered views: Equal Earth, Mercator, Equirectangular or Orthographic. Every Free Play set asks once about each projection in a shuffled order. Choose one of the four maps and check it to reveal the recommended match and its properties. In the constant-heading course task, the same orange route appears on every view so players can compare its shape. A correct answer earns 250 points, up to 1,000 across the four tasks. Task wording describes a common strength; it does not claim a projection preserves every map property or suits every scale and region. Map shapes use Maproom's bundled Natural Earth country boundaries and D3's local projection implementations. The game is Free Play only and remains outside Daily while prompt clarity and difficulty are evaluated.

## Population Pyramid Sort

In each of five Free Play rounds, reorder three anonymous country profiles from lowest to highest population share aged 65 or older. Each round uses a different trio, for 15 countries across a full set. Earlier/Later buttons support keyboard and touch. Each profile also has an optional age-band table with male and female shares rounded to 0.1 percentage point. The revealed 65+ share uses unrounded data, so adding the displayed values can differ slightly. Before answering, the game explains that the 65+ share combines both sides of the four oldest bands, from 65–69 through 80+. After checking, the result names the countries, shows their 65+ shares and awards 100 points for each correctly ordered pair, up to 1,500 points for the set. The mirrored pyramid bars show each age band's share of total population; the ordering target is the 2023 World Bank WDI 65+ indicator. The game uses a bundled offline dataset and remains outside Daily while first-read clarity and the five gap bands are evaluated. See the [source and rules review](research/competitive-landscape/STUDIES/population-pyramid-sort.md).

## Watershed Match

For each of five Free Play questions, choose which of three adjacent HUC12 units the 2025 USGS Watershed Boundary Dataset records as the source area's downstream destination. Each correct answer earns 100 points, up to 500 per set. The map provides location context but shows no river channels or flow direction; after checking, it highlights the recorded destination and draws a schematic relationship arrow. Every set includes all three sample areas: an Appalachian window, the Columbia and the Susquehanna. This limited source-link pilot works offline, remains outside Daily and appears in Explore's secondary specialized collection. Its decoys and first-read clarity still need editorial and player review; see the [source study](research/competitive-landscape/STUDIES/watershed-match-source-review.md) and [first-read pilot protocol](research/competitive-landscape/STUDIES/watershed-match-player-pilot.md).

## Local results and review

Each finished session records its game, mode, date or practice-session ID, score, correct/total count, and relevant settings. Daily results and interrupted sessions stay in IndexedDB with a localStorage fallback. Import/export includes resumable sessions. Statistics show per-game results and daily completion; country and flag practice can put previously missed places first. Progress stays in the current browser and does not synchronize between devices.
